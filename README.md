<h1>DD/MM/YYYY</h1>


Interface / Resources / AI 

- The strategic resources overview got an uplift and is just easier to read now.
- Country selection now shows whether a country has custom missions or high quality custom missions.
- Strategic resources system reworked internally and should no longer go into negatives. Factories short on resources stop and restart on their own.

- Rum AI no longer gets stuck previewing its Roman/Seljuk/Caliphate mission branch forever.
- AI is much more effective at upgrading its roads and no longer does it step-by-step, oops.
- AI is now more likely to complete missions that need a fort or building in one specific province by having a development focused option.
- AI is vastly better at building latent goods buildings and industrial factories when it has the resources. Previously, it absolutely failed to do anything with them as if they didn't even exist.

Flavour

- New government rank and ruler titles for many shattered tags.
- Forming Tibet or Himalayan empire gives more renaming options.
- Various flavour localisation changes to formable decision names and descriptions.
- Riga province and the Riga/Livonian primary culture are now baltic_german, as in vanilla.
- Renaming events: added grey historical explanations to the less obvious name options. Neat historical info to learn.
- New leader, ship, army and fleet names for many countries that shared or lacked their own list, report if any corrupted characters appear.
- Reforming into Livonia now gives some renaming options: Terra Mariana/Land of Mary if Christian, Livland if Germanic, Zemgale, Semigallia and Latvia.

Content

Generally, a focus on small countries that never had content. Small/medium sized missions with some additional side flavour to make them have an identity.

- Galicia and Volhynia: a shared mission tree with a column for each to be more flavourful.
- Farafra/Kemet: new mission trees, small/medium focused on Egypt and some surrounding area.
- Occitania: new extensive mission tree, events, and a government Paratge mechanic on formation.
- Mazovia (Vanilla bookmark): small mission tree as the Crown's vassal with a neat unique mechanic.
- Andalusi taifas (Sevilla, Algeciras, Alentejo): a shared mission tree with two columns for each taifa.
- Cumania: new mission tree integrated with the starting event. Go horse or become settled+religion pick.
- Norse faith Scandinavians get the Leidang military reform, which unlocks Caroleans without the Swedish missions.
- Burgundy: added the Europa Expanded mission tree as an option. Made primarily for the Vanilla bookmark, but Shattered should be viable after the start.
- Livonia: new decision for a Catholic Livonia to refound the Livonian Order (requires Lions of the North), choosing the crusader or Livonian path (with the Livonian government reforms).
- Sudetenland: added a small mission tree, ending in a choice to form Bohemia or remain Sudetenland (crusader theocracy, noble or peasant republic, or monarchy), both with new name options.
- Toledo: new mission tree and events. Toledo forms Spain through its last mission instead of the decision, without the administrative technology requirement and with an extra permanent bonus.
- Polish duchies (Wielkopolska, Mazovia, Kujawy, Malopolska, Krakow): a shared mission tree with its own column for each duchy. On the shattered start, forming Poland requires the Seniorate related to the missions.
- Fantasy Content: new "Shake the World" option. Lands get another culture and religion at the start, region based or true random. Likely to break or certain reforms/missions/formable requirements very difficult to meet. This is a meme.

Fixes/Tweaks

- Dhimmi temple agenda no longer picks non-state provinces.
- The Dream of Ruggero mission can now actually be unlocked.
- Norse and Geats culture provinces can now recruit Caroleans.
- Fixed the Ansbach Margrave War event declaring war on nobody.
- The Papal Orsini Border Dispute event now actually gives its claim.
- Fixed Evenk Siberian colonization event firing with no empty province left.
- Nationalism spread from same-culture neighbouring provinces now actually applies.
- Professional Army reform now gives 1.25 army professionalism per recruited general, not 2.25.
- Fixed newly gained Persian provinces not getting the Timurid Persian Empire province modifier.
- Fixed a crash when a mission gives a named flagship to a country that is not the original tag.
- Egyptian temple funding event and the entrepreneur Muslim school option now add their development.
- Massive error.log cleanup of events, decisions, the Persian Silk Route outposts and the Caliph mechanic.
- Sicilies/Two Sicilies internal mission flag shenanigans resulted in confusion. Confusion should be gone.
- Republican liberalism should be more consistent and appear as expected when the Age of Absolutism starts.
- Inca roads, Ardabil and other EE missions now show their hidden area development requirement in the tooltip.
- Excommunication penalties are now removed by the EE Austria/Castile/Burgundy missions that promise it, oopsie.
- Ducal Prussia (released as a march by the Polish missions) now gets its own mission tree instead of the generic one.
- Teutonic Holy Horde capital modifier now also works with any other religion's idea group, not just vanilla Religious Ideas.
