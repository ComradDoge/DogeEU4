# Kemet - the Black Land restored
# Tree for Kemet (EGP), formed by a Kemetic Egyptian country (decisions/_DST_FormAncientEgypt.txt), usually Farafra
# (missions/_DST_Farafra_Missions.txt). Replaces the old Antiquity Reborn tree.
# Slots: 1 The Gods, 2 The Two Lands, 3 The South, 4 The Sea, 5 The Horizon.
# The Faith of the River (kemet_path_maat / kemet_path_neteru) is chosen in Farafra's tree, or here in
# egp_the_black_land_whole by a Kemet that never chose.
# Events: events/_DSE_flavour_Kemet.txt (egp_events), events/_DSE_flavour_Kemet_Faith.txt (kemet_events)
# Modifiers: common/event_modifiers/DST_Kemet_modifiers.txt, effects: common/scripted_effects/DST_Kemet_scripted_effects.txt
# Triggers: common/scripted_triggers/_DST_Kemet_triggers.txt, Houses decisions: decisions/DST_Kemet_Houses.txt
# Monuments (Leviathan): kemet_valley_of_the_kings, kemet_deir_el_medina, kemet_great_library in common/great_projects/DST_monuments.txt.
# The suez_canal build_trigger (common/great_projects/00_great_projects.txt) accepts egp_the_canal_of_the_pharaohs.
