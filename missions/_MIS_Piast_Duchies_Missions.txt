# Piast duchies - the Seniorate
# One tree for the five Polish successor states (WPK, MAZ, KJW, MPK, KRA). Slot 5 is left to generic missions.
# Column 1 swaps per duchy (its identity and its route to the Seniorate). Columns 2 to 4 are shared.
# Seniorate: decisions/_DST_PiastSeniorate_Decisions.txt, common/scripted_triggers/DST_Piast_triggers.txt,
# common/scripted_effects/DST_Piast_scripted_effects.txt, on_war_won / on_yearly_pulse in common/on_actions/DST_on_actions.txt
# Events: events/_DSE_flavour_Piast_Duchies.txt (piast_events), modifiers: common/event_modifiers/DST_Piast_modifiers.txt
# Forming Poland (decisions/PolishNation.txt) ends this tree and the POL trees take over.
# Shared nodes never require a duchy-only mission: a required mission that isn't in the tree blocks completion.
# The link runs the other way instead: every duchy's D2 (its route to the Seniorate) requires piast_the_empty_throne.
