# Galicia and Volhynia - the Crown of Rus'
# One tree for the two Ruthenian successor states (GLA, VLH). Column 1 swaps per tag, columns 2 to 5 are shared.
# The coronation (gv_the_crown_of_rus) fires gv_events.3: the crown from Rome (gv_crown_of_rome) or the crown of
# Halicz (gv_crown_of_halicz). gv_the_church_of_rus, gv_the_polish_question, gv_the_lands_of_rus and gv_rex_russiae
# branch on that flag; their trigger falls back to "always = no" while no crown is chosen.
# Events: events/_DSE_flavour_Galicia_Volhynia.txt (gv_events), modifiers: common/event_modifiers/DST_Galicia_Volhynia_modifiers.txt
# Effects: common/scripted_effects/DST_Galicia_Volhynia_scripted_effects.txt, triggers: common/scripted_triggers/DST_Galicia_Volhynia_triggers.txt
# Shared nodes never require a tag-only mission: a required mission that isn't in the tree blocks completion.
# The link runs the other way: each tag's second node requires gv_the_romanovich_inheritance.
