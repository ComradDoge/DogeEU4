# Cumania (CUM) - the Cuman Path
# dse_flavour_cumania.1 (on_startup) sets the path: cum_path_horde, or settled with cum_faith_catholic /
# cum_faith_orthodox / cum_faith_bogomil (no faith flag = Tengri), then calls swap_non_generic_missions.
# Slots 1 and 2 are shared. Horde: slots 3 to 5 (cum_h_*). Settled: slots 3 and 4 (cum_s_*) and one faith series in slot 5.
# Shared nodes never require path nodes; faith nodes may require the settled Danube column (always present for them).
# Events: events/_DSE_flavour_Cumania.txt, effects: common/scripted_effects/DST_Cumania_scripted_effects.txt
# Triggers: common/scripted_triggers/DST_Cumania_triggers.txt, modifiers: common/event_modifiers/DST_Cumania_modifiers.txt
