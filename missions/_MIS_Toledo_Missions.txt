# Toledo - the city and the crown
# One tree for Toledo (TOL), from the start to Spain (decisions/SpanishNation.txt swaps it for the Spain trees).
# Slots: 1 The City, 2 The Crown of Castile, 3 The East, 4 The Frontier, 5 The Southern Question.
# tol_convivencia_or_crusade fires tol_events.2: the crusade (tol_crusade_path) or convivencia (tol_convivencia_path).
# The four nodes in slot 5 branch on that flag; their trigger falls back to "always = no" while no road is chosen.
# The crown of Castile is a name override (tol_crown_of_castile_effect), not a tag change, so this tree carries on.
# Events: events/_DSE_flavour_Toledo.txt (tol_events), modifiers: common/event_modifiers/DST_Toledo_modifiers.txt
# Effects: common/scripted_effects/DST_Toledo_scripted_effects.txt, triggers: common/scripted_triggers/DST_Toledo_triggers.txt
